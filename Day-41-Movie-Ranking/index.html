The Best Movies According to Me

My Top 3 Movies of All Time

Interstellar

One of my favourite sci-fi movies. I really like the story, visuals, music, and the way it explores space and time.

Inception

A brilliant movie with an interesting concept about dreams and reality. The plot keeps you thinking even after the movie ends.

The Dark Knight

One of the best superhero movies I've watched. The story, characters, and performances make it unforgettable.
