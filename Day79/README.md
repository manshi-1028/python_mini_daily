
# Day 79 — The Tragic Discovery of Handwashing: t-Tests & Distributions 🧼📊

## 100 Days of Code — Python Bootcamp

### 📚 What I Learned

Day 79 focused on **statistical analysis**, **distributions**, **histograms**, **Kernel Density Estimates (KDEs)**, and **t-tests**.

The project investigates the historical discovery made by **Dr. Ignaz Semmelweis**, who observed that handwashing dramatically reduced deaths among women giving birth in hospitals.

The main goal was to use data and statistical testing to investigate whether handwashing was associated with a reduction in deaths.
